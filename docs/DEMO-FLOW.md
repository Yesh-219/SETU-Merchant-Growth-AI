# SETU Hackathon Demonstration Flow & Script

This walkthrough outlines the 3-minute pitch and live demo flow of **SETU – AI-Powered Business Growth & Financial Intelligence Copilot for Merchants** for the **Paytm Build for India AI Hackathon – Hyderabad Finale**.

---

## 🎭 Persona & Context
- **Merchant:** Ramesh Sharma
- **Store Name:** Lakshmi Provision Store, Hyderabad
- **Profile:** Traditional Kirana & FMCG retailer processing 100+ transactions daily via Paytm Soundbox / QR code and cash.
- **Challenge:** Ramesh doesn't know his exact daily net profit margin, runs out of fast-moving products on weekends, and struggles to foresee if he can afford upcoming wholesale supplier invoices.

---

## 🎬 5-Step Live Demonstration Walkthrough

```
[1. Executive Dashboard] ────> [2. Sales Analytics] ────> [3. AI Inventory Alert]
                                                                    │
[5. Conversational Copilot] <──── [4. Cash Flow & Profit] <─────────┘
```

---

### Step 1: Merchant Executive Dashboard (`/`)

1. **Presenter Action**: Open the application at `http://localhost:5173/`.
2. **Visual Highlights**:
   - Clean Indian merchant UI in Paytm blue & cyan branding.
   - Header displaying *Lakshmi Provision Store* with **Paytm Verified** shield.
   - **4 Core KPI Cards**:
     - *30-Day Gross Revenue:* ₹2,84,500 (+14.8% growth).
     - *Total Orders:* 432 customer checkouts.
     - *Low Stock Items:* 5 products needing immediate attention.
     - *Net Profit:* ₹62,400 (21.9% margin).
   - **Active Alert Banners**:
     - Critical stock warning for Toor Dal.
     - Upcoming ₹15,400 supplier payment to Metro Cash & Carry.
     - Cooking oil demand surge.
   - **Recent Transactions Feed**: Live stream showing UPI and cash receipts.
3. **Presenter Script**:
   > *"Small merchants deal with hundreds of transactions every week, but at the end of the day, they often don't know whether they actually made a profit. SETU transforms raw checkout data into instant business clarity."*

---

### Step 2: Intelligent Sales Analytics (`/sales`)

1. **Presenter Action**: Click **Sales Analytics** in the sidebar.
2. **Visual Highlights**:
   - **Sales Trend Area Chart**: Displays the past 7 days of collection curves showing weekday dips and Saturday peak collections (₹18,900).
   - **Metrics Bar**: Today's Sales (₹12,450) and Average Order Value (₹658).
   - **Top Products Table**: Highlights highest grossing items (*Aashirvaad Atta 10kg*, *Tata Salt*, *Fortune Sunflower Oil*) with unit margins.
3. **Presenter Script**:
   > *"Here, Ramesh can see that his Saturday revenue spikes by nearly 60%. His average basket size is ₹658, driven primarily by 10kg flour bags and cooking oil pouches."*

---

### Step 3: AI Inventory & Demand Forecasting (`/inventory`)

1. **Presenter Action**: Click **AI Inventory** in the sidebar.
2. **Visual Highlights**:
   - **AI Demand & Restock Card (Gold & Amber highlight)**:
     - *Fortune Sunflower Oil 1L:* Only 4 left. AI notes a 32% demand increase and recommends restocking 30 units before the weekend rush.
     - *Toor Dal 1kg:* Only 2 left. Flagged with **HIGH URGENCY**.
   - **One-Click Action Button**: "Create Order" to draft a purchase order for suppliers.
   - **Full Inventory Master Table**: Visual badges showing Optimal, Low Stock, and Out of Stock states.
3. **Presenter Script**:
   > *"Instead of discovering an empty shelf when a customer asks for dal, SETU's predictive demand model alerts Ramesh 48 hours in advance, ensuring zero lost sales and minimal dead working capital."*

---

### Step 4: Profit & Cash Flow Intelligence (`/finance`)

1. **Presenter Action**: Click **Profit & Cash Flow** in the sidebar.
2. **Visual Highlights**:
   - **7-Day Cash Flow Projection Card**:
     - Projected liquidity balance: **₹41,200**.
     - Upcoming Payables timeline: Electricity bill (₹4,800 due Nov 18), Metro Cash & Carry Wholesale (₹15,400 due Nov 20), Store Rent (₹12,000 due Nov 25).
   - **P&L Breakdown Table**: Clear step-down from Gross Revenue (₹2,84,500) ➔ COGS (-₹2,04,800) ➔ Operating Overhead (-₹17,300) ➔ **Net Profit (₹62,400 / 21.9%)**.
3. **Presenter Script**:
   > *"Cash in the bank does not equal profit. SETU calculates true net margins by factoring in wholesale procurement costs, shop rent, and power bills. Ramesh can instantly see that his ₹41,200 projected cash comfortably covers his upcoming ₹15,400 supplier invoice on November 20th."*

---

### Step 5: Conversational AI Business Advisor (`/advisor`)

1. **Presenter Action**: Click **AI Advisor** in the sidebar.
2. **Visual Highlights**:
   - Interactive chat interface powered by **Sarvam AI**.
   - Initial greeting in conversational Hinglish/English addressing Ramesh ji.
   - Click a suggested prompt: *"Which products are about to go out of stock?"*
   - Immediate assistant response detailing low stock levels with interactive suggested action pills.
   - Type custom question: *"What is my estimated profit margin this month?"*
   - Copilot answers with exact net profit percentage and breakdown.
3. **Presenter Script**:
   > *"For a busy merchant, reading dashboards while attending to store customers is hard. With SETU's Sarvam AI-powered natural language copilot, Ramesh can simply ask questions in his own words and receive immediate, actionable business advice."*
